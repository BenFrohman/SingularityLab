# Never treat a lab cutoff as μ

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman (@BenFrohman)
Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman. Apache-2.0.

31 and 35 were produced. They are not μ.

Rules for this lab:

1. Never early-reduce. A `grlex` print that hides v^{11} is not the closed ideal.
2. Never divert with dummy variables or dummy μ.
3. Never allow a box cutoff to be the final datum. Incomplete until the isolated leading-term list is full and the count is stable for every larger box.
4. Never skip a leading term. For W^T the three LTs are u^4, u v^5, v^{11}. For W they are v^6, u^5, u^4 v.

Closed count:

    11 + 15 = 26 = μ(W^T)

Identity that forbids skipping v^{11}:

    v^5 (5u^4 + v^6) − 5 u^3 (u v^5) = v^{11}
