## Methods already in the literature

- Milnor number as dim C{x}/(df). Milnor-Orlik product for weighted isolated germs.
- Arnold ADE simple list. Inner modality = Jacobian monomials of degree >= d.
- Berglund-Hubsch-Krawitz transpose of invertible polynomials.
- Milnor fibration: bouquet of mu (n-1)-spheres.
- Ebeling-Gusein-Zade monodromy zeta duality: three-variable invertibles.
- FJRW / LG-CY (Chiodo-Ruan), Orlov matrix factorizations.

## What this lab supplies that those papers do not name

The explicit chain atom

    W = u^5 v + v^6,    W^T = u^5 + u v^6

with locked integers 25, 26, 30; inner modality 6 and 5; isolated LTs
u^4, uv^5, v^{11}; and the classification that F^T is a BHK string,
not a Fourier-Mukai fourfold and not a Hodge miss.

That is a worked example, not a new general theorem.
